## Similarity Metrics

### Cosine Similarity

$$
\cos(\theta) = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\| \times \|\mathbf{b}\|} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i b_i}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i^2} \times \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} b_i^2}}
$$

**Range**: [-1, 1] where 1 = identical direction

### Dot Product

$$
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i b_i
$$

**Note**: For normalized vectors, cosine = dot product

### Euclidean Distance

$$
d(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}) = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (a_i - b_i)^2}
$$

**Range**: [0, ∞) where 0 = identical

## BM25 (Sparse Retrieval)

### IDF (Inverse Document Frequency)

$$
\text{IDF}(t) = \ln\left(1 + \frac{N - \text{df}(t) + 0.5}{\text{df}(t) + 0.5}\right)
$$

Where:

- N = total documents
- df(t) = documents containing term t

### Full BM25 Score

$$
\text{BM25}(q, d) = \sum_{t \in q} \text{IDF}(t) \cdot \frac{\text{tf}(t,d) \cdot (k_1 + 1)}{\text{tf}(t,d) + k_1 \cdot \left(1 - b + b \cdot \frac{|d|}{\text{avgdl}}\right)}
$$

**Defaults**: k₁ = 1.5, b = 0.75

## Retrieval Metrics

### Recall@K

$$
\text{Recall@K} = \frac{|\text{Relevant} \cap \text{Retrieved@K}|}{|\text{Relevant}|}
$$

"What fraction of relevant docs did we find?"

### Precision@K

$$
\text{Precision@K} = \frac{|\text{Relevant} \cap \text{Retrieved@K}|}{K}
$$

"What fraction of retrieved docs are relevant?"

### F1-Score

$$
F_1 = 2 \times \frac{\text{Precision} \times \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}} = \frac{2 \times TP}{2 \times TP + FP + FN}
$$

### Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)

$$
\text{MRR} = \frac{1}{|Q|} \sum_{i=1}^{|Q|} \frac{1}{\text{rank}_i}
$$

Where rank_i is position of first relevant doc for query i

### DCG (Discounted Cumulative Gain)

$$
\text{DCG@K} = \sum_{i=1}^{K} \frac{\text{rel}_i}{\log_2(i + 1)}
$$

### nDCG (Normalized DCG)

$$
\text{nDCG@K} = \frac{\text{DCG@K}}{\text{IDCG@K}}
$$

Where IDCG = DCG of ideal ranking

### Average Precision (AP)

$$
\text{AP} = \frac{1}{R} \sum_{k=1}^{n} P(k) \cdot \text{rel}(k)
$$

Where R = total relevant docs, P(k) = Precision@k, rel(k) = 1 if doc k is relevant

### Mean Average Precision (MAP)

$$
\text{MAP} = \frac{1}{|Q|} \sum_{q=1}^{|Q|} \text{AP}(q)
$$

## RRF (Reciprocal Rank Fusion)

$$
\text{RRF}(d) = \sum_{r \in R} \frac{1}{k + r(d)}
$$

Where:

- R = set of rankers
- r(d) = rank of document d in ranker r
- k = smoothing constant (typically 60)

---

## ColBERT MaxSim

$$
\text{Score}(Q, D) = \sum_{i=1}^{|Q|} \max_{j=1}^{|D|} (\mathbf{q}_i \cdot \mathbf{d}_j)
$$

For each query token, find max similarity with any document token, then sum.

---

## MMR (Maximal Marginal Relevance)

$$
\text{MMR} = \arg\max_{d_i \in R \setminus S} \left[ \lambda \cdot \text{Sim}(d_i, q) - (1-\lambda) \cdot \max_{d_j \in S} \text{Sim}(d_i, d_j) \right]
$$

Where:

- λ = relevance vs diversity tradeoff (typically 0.5-0.7)
- S = already selected documents

## Cost Formulas

### Per-Query Cost

$$
\text{Cost} = \text{Embed}_{query} + \text{Rerank} + \text{LLM}_{input} + \text{LLM}_{output}
$$

### Embedding Cost

$$
\text{Cost} = \frac{\text{tokens}}{1,000,000} \times \text{rate}
$$

### LLM Cost

$$
\text{Cost} = \frac{\text{input\_tokens}}{1000} \times r_{in} + \frac{\text{output\_tokens}}{1000} \times r_{out}
$$
