## The Context vs Precision Trade-off

- **Small chunks (128 tokens)**: Great for retrieval precision. The vector is focused.
- **Large chunks (1024 tokens)**: Great for LLM comprehension. The model sees the surrounding context.

**Parent Document Retrieval** gives you the best of both worlds.

1.  Chunk your documents into small pieces (child chunks).
2.  Link each child to its parent (or a larger window around it).
3.  Index the **children**.
4.  When a child is hit, retrieve and send the **parent**.

## Implementation details

In this challenge, you simulate this by "expanding the window" around a hit index.
In production (LangChain/LlamaIndex), this is often called `ParentDocumentRetriever`.
