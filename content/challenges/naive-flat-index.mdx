<Callout title="Goal" variant="info">
  Implement a naive flat index (brute force search) for small-scale retrieval.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Before reaching for Faiss or Pinecone, understanding how to iterate and score vectors directly is key for:

- Debugging retrieval quality
- Implementing "reranking" or custom scoring
- Handling small datasets where an index overhead is unnecessary

## What you’re implementing

Given:

- `vectors: List[List[float]]`
- `query: List[float]`
- `k: int`

Return top `k` similar vectors.

This is fundamentally similar to "Embed and Search" but focuses on the data structure aspect (List of Lists).
