<Callout title="Goal" variant="info">
  Remove exact duplicate chunks safely using content hashes.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Duplicates are everywhere:

- repeated headers/footers in PDFs
- the same policy repeated across versions
- ingestion bugs causing re-indexing of identical content

If you don’t dedupe, you get:

- worse retrieval diversity
- redundant context (token waste)
- inflated index size and costs

## Offline vs online

- **Offline (ingestion)**: dedupe chunks before embedding/upserting.
- **Online (context shaping)**: dedupe retrieved candidates before reranking/packing.

## Production extension ideas

- near-duplicate dedup (SimHash/MinHash)
- store `content_sha1` in metadata
- track `doc_id` + `chunk_id` provenance for debugging
