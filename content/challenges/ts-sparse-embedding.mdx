## Why this matters in production

Sparse embeddings (TF-IDF, BM25 vectors) are the "S" in hybrid search. They excel at exact term matching where dense embeddings fail — like product codes or technical terms.

## What you're implementing

- Tokenize text and count term frequencies.
- Apply IDF weighting from corpus statistics.
- Return sparse vector (indices + values).

## Common pitfalls

- Not handling out-of-vocabulary terms.
- Forgetting to normalize by document length.

## After you pass

Combine with dense embeddings for **Hybrid Search**.
