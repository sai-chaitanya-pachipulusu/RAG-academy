## The "Specific Query" Problem

Users often ask highly specific questions: _"What was the exact revenue of the cloud division in Q3 2024?"_
Sometimes, a vector match for this exact string fails.

**Step-Back Prompting** (introduced by Google DeepMind) involves "stepping back" to ask a more general question first: _"What are the financial results for the cloud division in 2024?"_

## Why it works

Specific queries search for specific "needles". Step-back queries search for the "haystack" that likely contains the needle. By retrieving both, you significantly increase the chances of catching the right context.
