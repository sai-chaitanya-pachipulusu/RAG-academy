  Build a prompt injection detector to protect RAG systems from attacks.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Prompt injection is a top security concern for LLM applications. OWASP lists it as a critical vulnerability. Detecting and blocking injection attempts is essential.

## What you're implementing

- Detect patterns: "ignore instructions", "system:", "pretend", etc.
- Calculate confidence based on matches.
- Sanitize by removing suspicious patterns.

## Common pitfalls

- Too many false positives on normal queries.
- Missing Unicode variations of attack patterns.

## After you pass

Combine with rate limiting and monitoring for defense in depth.
