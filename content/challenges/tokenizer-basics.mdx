<Callout title="Goal" variant="info">
  Build a tiny tokenizer + token counter so you stop thinking in characters and start thinking in tokens.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Token budgets drive almost every critical RAG decision:

- chunk size
- candidate top‑k vs final top‑k
- prompt templates and safety instructions

If you can’t estimate tokens, you can’t reason about latency/cost/quality tradeoffs.

## What you’re implementing

A simple tokenization rule:

- lowercase
- keep sequences of `[a-z0-9]+`
- treat punctuation as separators

This is **not** a real tokenizer (BPE etc), but it builds the habit.

## Common pitfalls

- Not lowercasing consistently.
- Returning empty tokens.
- Trying to “preserve punctuation” (the lab explicitly treats it as separators).

## After you pass

Use token counting when implementing chunking and context packing.
