## Why this matters in production

Recall@K answers: "Of all relevant documents, what fraction did we retrieve in the top K?" It's the primary metric for RAG because if you don't retrieve relevant context, the LLM can't use it.

## What you're implementing

- Count relevant documents in top K results.
- Divide by total relevant documents.
- Return recall score (0 to 1).

## Common pitfalls

- Dividing by retrieved count instead of relevant count.
- Not handling empty relevant set (division by zero).

## After you pass

Implement **MRR** and **nDCG** for more nuanced retrieval evaluation.
