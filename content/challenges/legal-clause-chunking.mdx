  # Legal Clause Chunking

  Legal documents have strict structure: articles, clauses, sections, schedules. Splitting a clause mid-sentence can change legal meaning entirely!

  ## The Problem

  Standard chunking on legal text is dangerous:
  - **"The Buyer shall pay... [chunk break] ...upon delivery"** → Meaning lost
  - **Cross-references**: "per Section 3.2" must link to actual Section 3.2
  - **Definitions**: Terms defined in Section 1 used throughout

  ## Legal Document Structure

  ```
  ARTICLE I - DEFINITIONS
    Section 1.1. "Agreement" means...
    Section 1.2. "Party" means...

  ARTICLE II - OBLIGATIONS
    Section 2.1. The Buyer shall...
      (a) sub-clause...
      (b) sub-clause...
  ```

  ## Your Task

  Implement `legal_clause_chunk()` that:
  1. Detects ARTICLE patterns (ARTICLE I, ARTICLE 1, etc.)
  2. Detects Section patterns (Section 1.1, Section 2., etc.)
  3. Detects sub-clauses ((a), (b), (c), etc.)
  4. Tracks hierarchy: [Article I > Section 1.1 > (a)]

  ## Common Legal Patterns

  | Pattern | Regex | Purpose |
  |---------|-------|---------|
  | Articles | `^ARTICLE\s+[IVX]+` | Major divisions |
  | Sections | `^Section\s+\d+\.\d*` | Numbered clauses |
  | Sub-clauses | `^\([a-z]\)` | Lettered items |
  | Recitals | `^WHEREAS` | Background context |
  | Operative | `^NOW THEREFORE` | Binding provisions |

  ## Real-World Usage

  - **Harvey AI**: AI for legal analysis
  - **Kira Systems**: Contract review automation
  - **Luminance**: Due diligence platform
