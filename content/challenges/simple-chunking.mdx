## Why this matters in production

Chunking quality dominates retrieval quality for most RAG systems.

Even if you later use:

- header-aware chunking
- semantic chunking
- proposition chunking

…you still need a rock-solid baseline to compare against.

## What you’re implementing

- Split raw text into contiguous slices of size `chunk_size`
- Preserve text exactly (no trimming)
- Validate parameters (`chunk_size > 0`)

## Common pitfalls

- Off-by-one errors at the end of the string.
- Accidentally trimming whitespace (this changes hashes/traceability later).
