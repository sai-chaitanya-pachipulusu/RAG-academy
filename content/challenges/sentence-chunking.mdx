# Sentence-based Chunking

Character-based chunking can split words and sentences in half. **Sentence-based chunking** preserves complete thoughts.

### The Problem with Character Chunking

```
"Dr. Smith works at NASA. He leads the AI team."
# Character split at position 25:
Chunk 1: "Dr. Smith works at NASA."
Chunk 2: " He leads the AI team."
```

This is fine, but:

```
# Character split at position 20:
Chunk 1: "Dr. Smith works at N"
Chunk 2: "ASA. He leads the AI team."
```

This is broken!

### Your Task

1. Split text into sentences using `.!?` as delimiters.
2. Group sentences into chunks of `max_sentences`.

### Why It Matters

LlamaIndex's `SentenceSplitter` uses this approach. It ensures that each chunk represents a complete, coherent idea—improving embedding quality and retrieval relevance.
