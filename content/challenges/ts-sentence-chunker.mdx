<Callout title="TypeScript Challenge" variant="info">
  Implement a sentence-aware chunker that respects sentence boundaries.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Cutting text mid-sentence creates chunks with incomplete ideas. Sentence-aware chunking produces more coherent chunks that embed better and retrieve more accurately.

## What you're implementing

- Split text into sentences (by `.`, `!`, `?`).
- Group sentences into chunks of `maxSentences`.
- Support sentence-level overlap for context.

## Common pitfalls

- Splitting on `.` inside abbreviations (e.g., "Dr. Smith").
- Creating single-sentence chunks that are too small.

## After you pass

Explore semantic chunking, which groups by meaning rather than sentence count.
