  Decompose complex queries into simpler sub-queries.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Complex multi-part questions are hard to retrieve for. Decomposition breaks them into pieces that can be retrieved independently and answers synthesized.

## What you're implementing

- Detect query type: simple, compound, comparison, temporal.
- Split compound queries on conjunctions.
- Generate sub-queries for comparison questions.

## Common pitfalls

- Over-decomposing simple queries.
- Losing context between sub-queries.

## After you pass

Use decomposition with **Multi-Query Retrieval** for better coverage.
