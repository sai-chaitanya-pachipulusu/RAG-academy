## Why this matters in production

nDCG considers both relevance and position: highly relevant documents at the top matter more. It's the standard for benchmarks like BEIR and MTEB.

## What you're implementing

- DCG = Σ relevance[i] / log2(i + 2).
- IDCG = DCG with perfect ranking (sorted by relevance).
- nDCG = DCG / IDCG.

## Common pitfalls

- Using log2(i + 1) instead of log2(i + 2) (i is 0-indexed).
- Not handling IDCG = 0 (all relevance scores are 0).
