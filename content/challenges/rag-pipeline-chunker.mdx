## What is a Saga?

In RAG Academy, a **Saga** is a multi-step project where individual challenges build toward a complete, production-ready system.

The **RAG Pipeline Saga** will take you through the entire lifecycle of a request, from document ingestion to response generation.

## Stage 1: The Chunker

Your pipeline begins with raw text. To search it efficiently, you must divide it into digestible chunks.

In this challenge, you will implement the `chunk` method of a `Pipeline` class. This class will eventually host all your RAG logic.
