<Callout title="Goal" variant="info">
  Validate that citations in an answer only refer to provided sources: no `[0]`, no out-of-range `[99]`.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

If you show citations but don’t validate them, you get:

- fake trust (“looks grounded” but isn’t)
- broken UI links
- increased hallucination risk

This lab implements a minimal, deterministic validator.

## Production extensions (optional)

- require at least one citation for factual answers
- require citations per paragraph/claim
- enforce quote-attribution (citations attached to exact spans)
