## Why this matters in production

Rate limiting is essential for cost control and fair usage. Without it, a single user can exhaust your API budget or degrade service for others.

## What you're implementing

- Token bucket: holds up to maxTokens, refills over time.
- tryAcquire: check and deduct if available.
- acquire: wait until tokens available.

## Common pitfalls

- Not refilling based on elapsed time.
- Integer overflow on long-running systems.
