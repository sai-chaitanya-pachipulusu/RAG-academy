<Callout title="Goal" variant="info">
  Implement Inverted File (IVF) indexing to speed up search.
</Callout>

## Why this matters

Flat search is O(N). IVF reduces search space by clustering vectors and searching only relevant variable lists (O(N/C)).

## Task

1. Train K-Means centroids.
2. Assign vectors to centroids (inverted lists).
3. Search: Find nearest centroid, scan its list.
