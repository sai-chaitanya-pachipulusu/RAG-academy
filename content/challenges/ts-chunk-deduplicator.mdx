<Callout title="TypeScript Challenge" variant="info">
  Remove near-duplicate chunks from retrieval results.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Redundant chunks waste context window space and can confuse the LLM. Deduplication ensures each piece of context adds unique information.

## What you're implementing

- Compare chunks pairwise using similarity function.
- Remove duplicates above similarity threshold.
- Keep highest-scoring version of duplicates.

## Common pitfalls

- O(n²) complexity for large result sets.
- Setting threshold too low (aggressive deduplication).

## After you pass

Apply deduplication to your RAG pipeline before context building.
