## Why this matters in production

Top-k search is what every vector database does: find the k most similar documents to a query. This exact pattern runs millions of times per day at Pinecone, Weaviate, and Qdrant.

## What you're implementing

- Compute similarity between query and all documents.
- Sort by similarity score (descending).
- Return the top k results with scores.

## Common pitfalls

- Sorting in wrong order (ascending vs descending).
- Not handling `k > documents.length`.
