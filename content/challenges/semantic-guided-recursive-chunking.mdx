  # Semantic-Guided Recursive Chunking


  Recursive chunking respects structure but ignores semantics. Semantic chunking
  ignores structure. Why not use both?


  ## The Insight


  - **Recursive chunking**: Good at respecting document structure (headings,
  paragraphs)

  - **Semantic chunking**: Good at finding topical boundaries

  - **Combined approach**: Structure-first, then refine semantically


  ## The Algorithm


  1. **Phase 1: Structural Split** - Apply recursive chunking on structural
  boundaries

  2. **Phase 2: Coherence Check** - Compute internal coherence of each chunk

  3. **Phase 3: Semantic Refinement**
     - Low coherence → Split at internal semantic boundary
     - High similarity with neighbor → Consider merging
  4. **Result**: Chunks that respect both structure AND semantics


  ## Your Task


  Implement `semantic_guided_recursive_chunk()` that:

  1. Applies recursive structural splitting

  2. Computes coherence for each initial chunk

  3. Splits incoherent chunks at semantic boundaries

  4. Returns refined chunks with coherence metadata


  ## Why This Works


  Structure gives us good initial boundaries (paragraph breaks, section
  headings). But sometimes:

  - A single paragraph covers two topics (split it!)

  - Two paragraphs are really one topic (merge consideration)


  Semantic analysis refines the structural boundaries based on actual content
  similarity.
