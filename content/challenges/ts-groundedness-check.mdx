  Build a groundedness checker to verify LLM claims against sources.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Groundedness checking prevents hallucinations by verifying that LLM claims are supported by retrieved context. This is how production RAG systems build trust.

## What you're implementing

- Split response into individual claims.
- Check each claim against source documents.
- Calculate overall groundedness score.
- List unsupported claims for review.

## Common pitfalls

- Splitting on punctuation inside quotes.
- Setting similarity threshold too high (false negatives).

## After you pass

Use this in your RAG pipeline to flag potentially hallucinated content.
