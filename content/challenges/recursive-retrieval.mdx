## The "Needle in a Haystack" problem

If you have 1 million chunks, your retriever might miss the forest for the trees.
**Recursive Retrieval** solves this by creating a hierarchy:

1.  **Summarize** groups of chunks (e.g., a whole page or section).
2.  Index the **summaries**.
3.  Retrieve the best summary.
4.  Expand it into the **raw chunks** associated with that summary.

This is excellent for questions like _"What does the Q3 report say about revenue?"_ where a single chunk might not capture the full "revenue" context, but the section summary does.
