## Why this matters in production

Prompt templates are *not* “prompt engineering fluff” in RAG.

They are:

- a **contract** for grounding (“only use provided context”)
- a **format** for traceability (citations like `[1]`)
- the first line of defense against hallucination

## What you’re implementing

A plain string template that contains:

- the question
- numbered context chunks `[1]`, `[2]`, …
- explicit instruction to cite sources

## Common pitfalls

- Forgetting to number chunks (no stable citation mapping).
- Not explicitly instructing citation format.
