  Implement Maximal Marginal Relevance for result diversification.

## Why this matters in production

Top-k results often contain redundant information. MMR balances relevance with diversity to give users more complete coverage of a topic.

## What you're implementing

- MMR score = λ * relevance - (1-λ) * max_similarity_to_selected.
- Greedily select documents with highest MMR.
- Balance with λ parameter (1 = pure relevance, 0 = max diversity).

## Common pitfalls

- Computing similarity to all documents instead of just selected.
- Not pre-computing query similarities.
