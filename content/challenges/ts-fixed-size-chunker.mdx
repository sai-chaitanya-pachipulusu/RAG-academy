## Why this matters in production

Before you can search documents, you need to split them into chunks that fit in embedding model context windows. Fixed-size chunking is the simplest and most common approach.

LlamaIndex and LangChain both default to this strategy. Understanding it helps you tune chunk sizes for your use case.

## What you're implementing

- Split text into chunks of `chunkSize` characters.
- Support `overlap` for context continuity between chunks.
- Track `startIndex` and `endIndex` for each chunk (useful for citations).

## Common pitfalls

- Infinite loops when `overlap >= chunkSize`.
- Off-by-one errors with the last chunk.
