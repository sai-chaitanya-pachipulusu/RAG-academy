<Callout title="Goal" variant="warning">
  Redact common PII patterns from context **before** sending anything to an LLM.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Even if a user is authorized, you often need to avoid sending raw PII to third-party model providers.

This lab implements a basic deterministic redactor for:

- emails
- phone numbers
- SSN-like patterns

## Production extensions (optional)

- use a dedicated PII detector (Microsoft Presidio) for higher recall
- domain-specific patterns (account numbers, IBAN, PAN, health IDs)
- reversible redaction for internal-only providers (tokenization + secure vault)
