<Callout title="Goal" variant="info">
  Order context to mitigate “lost in the middle” by placing the best evidence at the beginning and end.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Even with long-context models, attention isn’t uniform:

- earliest tokens get more attention
- latest tokens get more attention
- the middle can be ignored

So if your #2 best chunk is buried in the middle, the model may miss it.

## Where this fits

This is a **post-retrieval context shaping** step:

1. retrieve + (optional) rerank → scored candidates
2. dedupe / diversify
3. **order for attention**
4. pack under token budget
5. generate with citations
