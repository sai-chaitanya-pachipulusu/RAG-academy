# Sparse Vector (Bag of Words)

While dense embeddings capture semantic meaning, **sparse vectors** represent text as a dictionary of word frequencies. This is the foundation of BM25 and hybrid search.

### Dense vs. Sparse

- **Dense**: `[0.12, -0.05, 0.88, ...]` (every dimension has a value)
- **Sparse**: `{"apple": 2, "banana": 1}` (only present words have values)

### Why Sparse Matters

A query for "Error 504" will semantically match "HTTP issues" in dense space, but might miss the exact document that says "Error 504". Sparse search guarantees exact keyword matches.
