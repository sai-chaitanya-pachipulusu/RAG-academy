<Callout title="Goal" variant="warning">
  Decide when to answer vs refuse when context is insufficient.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

The most dangerous failure mode is confident hallucination.

A refusal policy is a **safety valve**:

- if retrieval fails, you refuse or ask a follow-up question
- you don’t “fill in the gaps” from model priors

This lab uses a simple overlap gate to teach the concept.

## Production extensions (optional)

- use calibrated retrieval thresholds
- add a “need more info” follow-up question generator
- log refusal rates and investigate spikes (retrieval regressions)
