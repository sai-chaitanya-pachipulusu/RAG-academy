  Extract and validate citations from LLM responses.

## Why this matters in production

Citations let users verify RAG answers against sources. Extracting and validating them enables trustworthy AI that shows its work.

## What you're implementing

- Find citation patterns like [1], [2] in text.
- Track positions for highlighting.
- Validate that cited indices exist in source list.
- Create clean text without citation markers.

## Common pitfalls

- Not handling [10]+ (multi-digit citations).
- Missing edge cases like [0] or negative numbers.

## After you pass

Use this to build a grounded QA system with source verification.
