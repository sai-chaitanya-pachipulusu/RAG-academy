## Why this matters in production

Inverted indexes power Elasticsearch, Lucene, and every text search engine. Understanding this is foundational for hybrid RAG where you combine keyword and semantic search.

## What you're implementing

- Build: map terms → list of (docId, positions).
- Track document lengths for BM25 scoring.
- Search: find documents containing all query terms.

## Common pitfalls

- Not handling case sensitivity.
- Forgetting to track term positions for phrase queries.

## After you pass

Use your inverted index with **BM25 Scoring** for full-text search.
