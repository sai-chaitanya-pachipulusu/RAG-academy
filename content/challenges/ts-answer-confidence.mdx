  Score confidence in RAG answers based on retrieval quality.
</Callout>

## Why this matters in production

Users need to know when to trust RAG answers. Confidence scoring surfaces this, enabling appropriate caveats and human review triggers.

## What you're implementing

- Retrieval quality: average of retrieval scores.
- Source consistency: variance of scores.
- Coverage: number of supporting sources.
- Overall score: weighted combination.

## Common pitfalls

- Not surfacing low confidence to users.
- Overconfident on single high-scoring source.

## After you pass

Use confidence scores to trigger human review for critical applications.
