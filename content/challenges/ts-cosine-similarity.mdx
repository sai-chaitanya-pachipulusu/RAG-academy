  Implement cosine similarity — the standard metric for comparing embedding vectors.

## Why this matters in production

Cosine similarity is the default metric in most vector databases because it's scale-invariant. A short document and a long document can be fairly compared because we only measure the angle between vectors, not their magnitude.

## What you're implementing

- Compute dot product of two vectors.
- Compute magnitude (L2 norm) of each vector.
- Return `dot(a, b) / (||a|| * ||b||)`.
- Handle zero-magnitude vectors (throw Error).

## Common pitfalls

- Division by zero when a vector has all zeros.
- Forgetting to take the square root for magnitude.
