## Beyond Vector Search

Vector search is great at finding "similar" text, but it fails at complex reasoning like _"Which company does Alice work for and what do they produce?"_ if the information is spread across multiple chunks.

**GraphRAG** solves this by:

1.  Extracting **Entities** (Alice, Apple, Company).
2.  Extracting **Relations** (Alice -> WORKS_AT -> Apple).
3.  Querying the resulting graph.

## Task

Implement a basic triple extractor that identifies relationships based on predefined sentential patterns.
