## Stage 2: The Embedder

Now that you have chunks, you need to turn them into numbers. A production pipeline typically caches these embeddings or uses a consistent API.

## Task

Extend your `Pipeline` class with an `embed` method. This method will take your chunks and generate high-dimensional vectors that capture their semantic meaning.
