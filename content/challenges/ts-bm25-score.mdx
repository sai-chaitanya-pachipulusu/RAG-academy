## Why this matters in production

BM25 powers Elasticsearch, Lucene, and most traditional search. In hybrid RAG systems, BM25 handles exact keyword matching where dense embeddings fail (like product codes or technical terms).

## What you're implementing

- Compute IDF (inverse document frequency) for query terms.
- Compute length-normalized term frequency.
- Apply the BM25 formula with k1 and b parameters.

## Common pitfalls

- Forgetting the `+1` in IDF to avoid negative scores.
- Not normalizing by document length.
