## Why this matters in production

ReAct (Reasoning + Acting) is the foundational pattern for LLM agents. It's used in LangChain agents, AutoGPT, and Claude's tool use.

## What you're implementing

- Think: generate thought and decide on action.
- Act: execute the action and get observation.
- Loop: repeat until action is null or max steps reached.
- Return: final answer and execution trace.

## Common pitfalls

- Infinite loops without max steps limit.
- Not preserving action history for context.
